Each cell is a reel of flaps that only turns one way, so a letter flips through every character between where it is and where it's going. The top half falls with gravity and darkens as it turns away from the light; the bottom half swings down onto the stop. Long journeys speed up so no cell takes more than 1.6 seconds, and the last flip lands slower with a small bounce.

Pass `words` to cycle through, or `value` to show a fixed string. The board pauses while the tab is hidden. With reduced motion, letters change without flipping.
