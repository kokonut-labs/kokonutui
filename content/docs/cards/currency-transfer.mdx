## Usage

With no props the card runs a demo: the payment is taken, converted, sent and arrives on its own. Pass `status` to drive it from your own transfer state. While `status` is `"processing"` the steps run up to the last one and wait there.

```tsx
<CurrencyTransfer
  from={{ amount: 1200, currency: "GBP", symbol: "£", bank: "Monzo", last4: "1180" }}
  to={{ amount: 1404, currency: "USD", symbol: "$", bank: "Chase", last4: "4589", name: "Sam Carter" }}
  rate={1.17}
  txnId="TXN-8F2K19QZ"
  timestamp="2:30 PM"
  status={state}
  onRetry={retry}
/>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `from` | `TransferAccount` | 500.00 USD, Chase ••4589 | Sending account and amount. |
| `to` | `TransferAccount` | 460.00 EUR, Lena Fischer, Deutsche Bank ••7823 | Receiving account and amount. |
| `rate` | `number` | `0.92` | Units of `to.currency` per one `from.currency`. |
| `txnId` | `string` | `"TXN-DAB3UL494"` | Reference shown in the footer, with a copy button. |
| `timestamp` | `string` | `"10:45 AM"` | When the rate was locked. |
| `status` | `"processing" \| "completed" \| "failed"` | — | Controlled status. Omit it for the self-running demo. |
| `locale` | `string` | `"en-US"` | BCP 47 locale for amounts, currency symbols and the rate. |
| `onRetry` | `() => void` | — | Shows a Retry button on the step that failed. |
| `className` | `string` | — | Extra classes for the card. |

`TransferAccount` is `{ amount: number; currency: string; symbol?: string; bank: string; last4?: string; name?: string }`. The receiving `name` labels the card and its initials avatar. Status changes are announced to screen readers, and with reduced motion the digits, rail and ticks jump to their state.
