Grab any corner and pull. The fold is real geometry: the line between the corner and your finger is folded over, the sticker keeps one side and the other flips over to show its back, casting a shadow on the face. Let go early and it springs back flat. Pull past halfway, or flick it, and it turns over and comes away, revealing what was underneath.

The bottom-right corner sits slightly lifted as a hint, and lifts a little more on hover. With the keyboard, focus the sticker and press Enter.
