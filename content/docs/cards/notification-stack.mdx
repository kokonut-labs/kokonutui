Hover the stack (or tap it on a touch screen) to fan it out. Swipe a card sideways to dismiss it: either drag it past 100px or flick it, since a fast swipe dismisses whatever the distance. Vertical drags scroll the page as usual. With the keyboard, focus a card and press Delete.
