Pick a card up and throw it. It slides with friction and bounces off the edges of the board. Throw it by a corner and it spins; throw it by the middle and it just slides. While you hold it, it swings a little behind your hand like a sheet of paper. Grab a card mid-slide to catch it.

The card you pick up comes to the top. **Tidy up** lays everything out in a neat grid. With the keyboard, focus a card and use the arrow keys (Shift moves further).
