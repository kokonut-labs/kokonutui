Each column is a real 3D cylinder: rows sit round it with `rotateX()` and `translateZ()`, and the whole drum turns with one transform. Flick a column and it spins, slows down and lands exactly on a row. Hours and minutes loop forever; AM/PM stops at its ends with a rubber-band bounce. Tap a row to turn to it, or use the scroll wheel.

The drum is drawn twice: a muted copy, and a full-color copy clipped to the selection band, so each row turns dark exactly as it crosses the band's edge. With the keyboard, focus a column and use the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End.
